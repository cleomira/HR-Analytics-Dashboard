# HR-Analytics-Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI to analyze workforce trends, employee attrition, and organizational demographics. The dashboard provides HR teams and business stakeholders with actionable insights into employee distribution, attrition patterns, salary impact, job satisfaction, and workforce composition.

The goal of this project is to support data-driven HR decision-making by transforming raw employee data into meaningful visual insights through interactive reporting and analytics.

---

 🎯 Objectives
The primary objectives of this dashboard are to:

- Monitor employee attrition and retention trends
- Analyze workforce demographics and age distribution
- Identify departments and job roles with high attrition
- Understand the relationship between salary, experience, and attrition
- Support strategic HR planning and workforce management

---
🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Power BI** — Dashboard development and data visualization
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — KPI calculations and measures
- **CSV Dataset** — HR employee dataset used for analysis

---

📊 Dashboard Features

KPI Cards
The dashboard includes key HR metrics such as:
- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Experience

---

 Workforce & Attrition Analysis
The report provides insights into:
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Salary Slab
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Job Role & Satisfaction
- Attrition Trend by Experience
- Department-wise Employee Count
- Employee Age Group Distribution

---
 ✨ Interactive Features
- Dynamic slicers and filters
- Department-based filtering
- Age group segmentation
- Interactive visualizations
- Cross-filtering between charts
- Responsive dashboard layout

---

 📈 Key Insights
- The organization recorded an attrition rate of **16.3%**.
- Employees within lower salary slabs experienced higher attrition levels.
- Certain departments contributed significantly more to attrition than others.
- The workforce is primarily concentrated within the **26–35** age group.
- Male employees represented a larger proportion of total attrition compared to female employees.
- Attrition trends varied across experience levels and job roles.
